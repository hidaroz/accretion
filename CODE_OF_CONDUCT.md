# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be decent to people. Assume good faith, critique the work rather than the
person, and accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no.

Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments aren't welcome here and will get you
removed from the project's spaces.

Report problems to the maintainer through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/hidaroz/accretion/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue if it isn't sensitive. Reports are handled privately.
